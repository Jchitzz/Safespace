Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026 — this is a starting template, not finished legal copy. See the note at the very bottom before you publish this.

SafeSpace is not therapy, counseling, medical advice, or a crisis service. It connects you with anonymous peers for informal support conversations. The people you talk to are not verified professionals and their advice is not vetted.

If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, please contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org), text HOME to 741741 for the Crisis Text Line, or contact your local emergency number. Outside the US, find a local helpline at findahelpline.com.

1. Who can use SafeSpace

You must be 18 years of age or older to use this site. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create a session, and we do not verify age — by using SafeSpace you are personally representing that you meet this requirement. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will take reasonable steps to remove their access.

2. What this service is (and isn't)

SafeSpace pairs you with another anonymous user for a real-time text conversation, either to vent or to listen. We do not screen, credential, train, or supervise the people you're matched with. Nothing said in a SafeSpace conversation is professional medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice, and you should not rely on it as such.

3. Acceptable use

By using SafeSpace, you agree not to:

  • Share your or anyone else's real name, contact information, address, or other identifying details
  • Harass, threaten, abuse, or attempt to manipulate another user
  • Use the service to solicit, advertise, or promote anything commercial
  • Impersonate a licensed professional or claim to provide clinical treatment
  • Post or transmit content that is illegal, sexually explicit involving minors, or otherwise violates the law
  • Attempt to record, screenshot, or redistribute a conversation without the other person's consent
  • Attempt to disrupt, reverse-engineer, or abuse the underlying service (e.g., spamming, scripting bulk connections)

Any user can report a conversation. Reports are reviewed and may result in access being restricted for the associated IP address, either automatically after multiple reports or manually by an administrator, though as a peer-matching service we cannot guarantee real-time moderation of every conversation. We're upfront that IP-based restriction has real limits — it can be bypassed by switching networks or using a VPN — and treat it as one layer of abuse prevention, not a guarantee that a specific person can never return.

4. No warranty; limitation of liability

SafeSpace is provided "as is," without warranties of any kind. We do not guarantee that conversations will be helpful, appropriate, accurate, or safe. To the fullest extent permitted by law, SafeSpace and its operators are not liable for any harm, loss, or damages arising from your use of the service or from the conduct of another user, including anything said to you in a conversation. You use this service, and act on anything discussed in it, at your own risk.

5. Data we collect

SafeSpace does not require an account, email address, or real name. Specifically:

  • Each visit is assigned a random, temporary identifier and a randomly generated display name (e.g. "Quiet Fox") that is not linked to your real identity
  • Conversation messages are relayed live between the two matched users and are not stored in a database once your session ends
  • If you submit a report, we store the reason, optional details, a timestamp, which anonymous session it relates to, and the IP address of the person being reported (not yours, if you're the one reporting) — this is used only to prevent repeat abuse, as described below, not to identify anyone personally
  • We use each participant's IP address at connection time to check it against a list of temporarily or permanently restricted IPs, and to detect when the same IP is being reported repeatedly in a short window. IP addresses are not linked to your anonymous display name, shown to other users, or retained beyond what's needed for this abuse-prevention system
  • Standard web server logs (e.g. IP address, browser type) may be temporarily retained by our hosting provider for security purposes
  • We use browser local storage only to remember that you've acknowledged this policy, so you aren't asked again on every visit

6. How we use data

We use the limited data described above solely to operate the matching and chat service, respond to reports, and prevent abuse (e.g., rate-limiting). We do not sell personal data, and we do not run advertising on this site.

7. Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13, and no one under 18 is permitted to use it (see Section 1). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update this page from time to time. Continued use of SafeSpace after a change means you accept the updated terms.
